Drop any image (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, `.gif`) into this **Images/Banner/** folder and it becomes your dashboard banner. If there are several, the dashboard uses the first by name — or pick one explicitly in the dashboard's ⚙️ **Settings**.

You can also upload one straight from ⚙️ Settings without leaving Obsidian.

In **Match image** theme mode, the dashboard rebuilds its colors from whatever banner you choose here.
