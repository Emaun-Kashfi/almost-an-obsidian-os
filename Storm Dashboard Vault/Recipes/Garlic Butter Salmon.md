# Garlic Butter Salmon

[[🍽️ Recipe Index|← Recipes]]

## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon
- Fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Season salmon with salt and pepper.
2. Sear skin-side down 4 minutes, flip, cook 3 more.
3. Add butter and garlic, spoon over the fillets.
4. Finish with lemon and parsley.
