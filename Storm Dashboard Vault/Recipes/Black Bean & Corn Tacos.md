# Black Bean & Corn Tacos

[[🍽️ Recipe Index|← Recipes]]

## Ingredients
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 cup corn
- 6 small tortillas
- 1 avocado
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp cumin
- Fresh cilantro

## Instructions
1. Warm beans and corn with cumin in a skillet.
2. Char tortillas over a flame or in a dry pan.
3. Fill with the bean mix, avocado, salsa, and cilantro.
4. Finish with a squeeze of lime.
