# Lentil Coconut Curry

[[🍽️ Recipe Index|← Recipes]]

## Ingredients
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp curry powder
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 tbsp oil

## Instructions
1. Sauté onion and garlic in oil until soft.
2. Stir in curry powder, then lentils, tomatoes, and coconut milk.
3. Simmer 20 minutes until lentils are tender.
4. Fold in spinach and serve over rice.
