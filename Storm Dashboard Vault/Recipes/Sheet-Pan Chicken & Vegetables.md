# Sheet-Pan Chicken & Vegetables

[[🍽️ Recipe Index|← Recipes]]

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb chicken thighs
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, wedged
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Toss chicken and vegetables with oil and spices on a sheet pan.
3. Roast 30–35 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
4. Rest 5 minutes and serve.
