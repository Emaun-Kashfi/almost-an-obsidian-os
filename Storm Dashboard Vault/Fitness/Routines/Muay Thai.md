# 🥊 Muay Thai

> Striking session — technique, combos and bag/pad work. Pull combos from your [[Muay Thai Combos]] and Evolve notes.

## Session
- [ ] Warm-up — skip rope · 3 × 3 min · load: —
- [ ] Shadow boxing (footwork + guard) · 3 × 3 min · load: —
- [ ] Technique / combos — see [[Muay Thai Combos]] · 4 × 3 min · load: —
- [ ] Heavy bag rounds · 5 × 3 min · load: —
- [ ] Clinch / knees · 3 × 2 min · load: —
- [ ] Conditioning finisher — teeps, push-ups, sit-ups · 2 rounds · load: —
- [ ] Stretch / cool-down · 5 min

## 🎬 Exercise demos

> Mostly technique work — tap **▶ video** for a demonstration. Combos live in [[Muay Thai Combos]].

**Jump Rope** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=boxing+jump+rope+for+beginners)
Light bounces on the balls of the feet, wrists turn the rope.

**Shadow boxing** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muay+thai+shadow+boxing+beginner)
Move on the balls of the feet, hands up, return to guard after every strike.

**Technique / combos** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muay+thai+combos+for+beginners)
Drill slow, then add speed.

**Teep (push kick)** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muay+thai+teep+push+kick+technique)
Knee up first, then extend and push through the ball of the foot; hands stay up.

**Jab–Cross** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=jab+cross+technique+boxing)
Rotate the hips into the cross, snap it back, chin down, guard tight.

**Heavy bag rounds** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muay+thai+heavy+bag+workout)
Full combos on the bag, stay light, breathe out on strikes.

**Clinch / knees** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=muay+thai+clinch+knees+basics)
Control the head and neck, drive the knees, stay balanced.

**Sit-Ups (finisher)** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=sit+up+proper+form)
Knees bent, curl up toward the knees, lower slow.
![[Fitness/demos/situp.gif|320]]

**Stretch / cool-down** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=post+workout+stretch+routine)
Ease down and breathe.
