# 🏃 Cardio & Conditioning

> Engine work — roadwork or intervals plus a short core circuit. Pick the main effort that fits the day.

## Session
- [ ] Warm-up — easy jog or row · 5 min · load: —
- [ ] Main effort — pick one: 30 min steady run · OR 6 × 400 m · OR 20 min HIIT · load: —
- [ ] Core circuit — hollow rocks, leg raises, plank · 3 rounds · load: —
- [ ] Cool-down walk + stretch · 5 min
