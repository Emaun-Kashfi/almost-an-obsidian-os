# 🏋️ Calisthenics Strength

> Full-body calisthenics for strength days — posterior chain and postural focus, MMA-relevant patterns. Full detail and form cues in [[Calisthenics Strength Plan]].

Edit the sets/reps/load to match where you're at. When you log this from your dashboard or daily note, this checklist is copied in so you can tick it off and record your numbers.

## Session
- [ ] Warm-up — arm circles, T-spine rotations, world's greatest stretch, hip circles, squats, inchworms, dead hang · 8 min
- [ ] Negative Pull-Ups · 2 × 3–5 · load: BW
- [ ] Australian Rows · 3 × 8–10 · load: BW
- [ ] Prone Y-T-W Raises · 2 × 8 each · load: —
- [ ] Superman Holds · 2 × 8 (2s hold) · load: —
- [ ] Push-Ups · 3 × 8–12 · load: BW
- [ ] Pike Push-Ups · 2 × 6–8 · load: BW
- [ ] Hollow Body Hold · 3 × 20s · load: —
- [ ] Side Plank · 2 × 20s each side · load: —
- [ ] Bodyweight Squats · 3 × 15 · load: BW
- [ ] Glute Bridges · 3 × 12 · load: BW
- [ ] Cool-down / mobility · 5 min

## 🎬 Exercise demos

> Follow-along animations made for this plan (the figure shows the movement pattern). Tap **▶ video** for a real-person demonstration.

**Warm-up** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=full+body+dynamic+warm+up+routine)
Arm circles, T-spine rotations, world's greatest stretch, hip circles, squats, inchworms, dead hang.

**Negative Pull-Ups** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=negative+pull+up+proper+form)
Jump or step to the top, then lower for 3–5s under control, full range.
![[Fitness/demos/neg_pullup.gif|320]]

**Australian Rows** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=australian+inverted+row+form)
Body dead straight, pull the chest to the bar, squeeze the shoulder blades.
![[Fitness/demos/australian_row.gif|320]]

**Prone Y-T-W Raises** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=prone+Y+T+W+raises+shoulder)
Face-down, thumbs up; lift the arms into a Y, then T, then W. Small and controlled.

**Superman Holds** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=superman+exercise+lower+back+form)
Lift chest, arms and legs off the floor, hold 2s, lower slow. Don't crank the neck.
![[Fitness/demos/superman.gif|320]]

**Push-Ups** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=push+up+proper+form)
Straight line head-to-heels, elbows ~45°, chest to the floor, press up fully.
![[Fitness/demos/pushup.gif|320]]

**Pike Push-Ups** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pike+push+up+form)
Hips high in an inverted-V, lower the crown of the head toward the floor, press up.
![[Fitness/demos/pike_pushup.gif|320]]

**Hollow Body Hold** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hollow+body+hold+form)
Low back pressed into the floor, arms and legs extended, ribs down.
![[Fitness/demos/hollow_hold.gif|320]]

**Side Plank** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=side+plank+proper+form)
Elbow under shoulder, stack the feet, hips up in a straight line; hold each side.
![[Fitness/demos/side_plank.gif|320]]

**Bodyweight Squats** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=bodyweight+squat+proper+form)
Sit back and down, knees track the toes, thighs to parallel, shin ∥ torso.
![[Fitness/demos/squat.gif|320]]

**Glute Bridges** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=glute+bridge+exercise+form)
Heels flat and close, drive the hips up, squeeze the glutes hard at the top.
![[Fitness/demos/glute_bridge.gif|320]]

**Cool-down / mobility** · [▶ video](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=post+workout+cool+down+stretch)
Easy stretching and slow breathing.
