Put audio files (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.flac`, `.opus`) in this **Audio/** folder and they show up in the Now Playing card on your dashboard, with play, pause, skip, and scrub, all offline.

Name a file `Artist - Title.mp3` and the card splits it into artist and title. See [[🎧 Now Playing]] for playlists and folder options.
